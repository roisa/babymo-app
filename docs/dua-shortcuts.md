# Doa Baby Mo in Shortcuts · Baby Mo du'a in Shortcuts

The iPhone app has a Shortcuts action, **Putar Doa Baby Mo · Play Baby Mo Du'a**
(`ios/App/App/DuaIntents.swift`). It plays Mo's recorded du'a out loud and then shows
the du'a text. Moments: riding, leaving home, entering home, before sleeping and
waking up. The action needs iOS 16 or later. It uses no location permission and no network.

## Riding du'a when the car connects (English)

1. Open the **Shortcuts** app → **Automation** → **+** (New Automation).
2. Choose **CarPlay** → **Connects** (no CarPlay? choose **Bluetooth** → pick your car).
3. Choose **Run Immediately** and turn off **Notify When Run** if you prefer.
4. **New Blank Automation** → **Add Action** → search **Baby Mo** →
   **Putar Doa Baby Mo · Play Baby Mo Du'a**.
5. Tap **Moment** and choose **Naik kendaraan · Riding** → **Done**.

When the car connects, Mo says the riding du'a through the car speakers, so the
driver never needs to look at the phone.

Other good automations:
- **Leave** (location: Home) → *Keluar rumah · Leaving home*
- **Arrive** (location: Home) → *Masuk rumah · Entering home*
- **Alarm** → *is stopped* (wake-up alarm) → *Bangun tidur · Waking up*
- **Sleep** → *Wind Down starts* or *Bedtime starts* → *Sebelum tidur · Before sleeping*

The location in "Leave"/"Arrive" is checked by iOS itself; Baby Mo never sees it.
You can also just say **"Hey Siri, Doa Baby Mo"** and pick the moment.

## Doa naik kendaraan saat mobil tersambung (Indonesia)

1. Buka app **Pintasan (Shortcuts)** → **Otomatisasi** → **+**.
2. Pilih **CarPlay** → **Tersambung** (tanpa CarPlay? pilih **Bluetooth** → pilih mobil).
3. Pilih **Jalankan Segera**.
4. **Otomatisasi Kosong Baru** → **Tambah Tindakan** → cari **Baby Mo** →
   **Putar Doa Baby Mo · Play Baby Mo Du'a**.
5. Ketuk **Momen** → pilih **Naik kendaraan · Riding** → **Selesai**.

Saat mobil tersambung, Mo membacakan doa naik kendaraan lewat speaker mobil.
Otomatisasi lain: **Pergi/Tiba di Rumah**, **Alarm dihentikan**, **Waktu tidur dimulai**.
Lokasi diperiksa oleh iOS sendiri, Baby Mo tidak pernah melihatnya.
